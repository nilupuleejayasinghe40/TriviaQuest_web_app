Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Is TriviaQuest free to use?

Yes! TriviaQuest is completely free. Create an account and start playing right away.

Do I need an account to take quizzes?

Yes, you need to sign up and log in to take quizzes. This allows us to save your scores and show you on the leaderboard.

How are scores calculated?

Your score is based on the percentage of correct answers. Each correct answer counts equally regardless of difficulty.

Can I suggest new questions?

Absolutely! Use the form above to send us your question suggestions and we'll review them for inclusion.
